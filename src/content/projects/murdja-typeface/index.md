Murdja is a display typeface drawing on Estonian folklore and the wilderness. Its sharp, irregular letterforms recall woodland undergrowth, with a nod to runic shapes. It suits projects with a rough, underground feel: zines, gig posters, band merch, theatre and craft branding.

The font is free for personal and commercial use. Credit is appreciated but not required.

Thanks to Mart Anderson, who runs the typography course at Pallas University of Applied Sciences.
