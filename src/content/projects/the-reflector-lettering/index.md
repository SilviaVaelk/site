The Reflector is a design by Sander Välk, built with Triomf for Into The Great Wide Open, a yearly festival on the island of Vlieland.

I lettered an instructional poem by Jan Goodrich for the installation's door, so the door itself explains what the space is for.

The process went from layout sketches, looking for a symmetrical composition with playful letter connections, to vector refinement in Illustrator with several test prints. The final lettering was laser-engraved into the door, which held up to the weather and felt friendlier than a painted or printed sign, then finished with varnish.
